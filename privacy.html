Privacy & credits

Your information

The project brief is prepared in your browser. Form entries are not automatically sent to Savior Productions or saved by this site. You choose whether to copy or download your brief.

Your turn behind the lens.

The camera studio asks for permission before using your camera. You can also choose a photograph from your device. Photo processing and downloads happen in your browser; photographs are not uploaded by this website. Closing the studio stops the camera.

Preferences and video

This site remembers your motion preference locally and whether you have seen the opening during this browsing session. It does not use advertising trackers. Playing the film loads YouTube’s privacy-enhanced player and connects to YouTube. External links have their own privacy policies.
