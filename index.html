Beth Rooney

Founder & CEO, Savior Productions

A life behind the scenes.
An eye for what matters.

Beth’s work moves between television, photography and film. An Emmy Award-winning sound engineer, producer and stage manager, she began her television career at MTV Networks.

Her television credits include Inside the Actors Studio, Maury Povich, Jerry Springer, The View and Who Wants to Be a Millionaire (Emmy Award-winning contributor).

She has served as stage manager from Live with Regis and Kelly through Live with Kelly and Mark (2000–current).

Her live-production credits include stage management for the 2023 MTV Video Music Awards and the TIME100 television specials (2022–2023), plus segment stage management for the 2022 MTV Video Music Awards.

She produced and executive produced Long Road Home (2012), with additional film work as a location assistant on Slapface (2021).

A Fashion Institute of Technology graduate, she brings a background in advertising and communications to her creative work.

Behind the camera, she photographs portraits, musicians, weddings and film sets. Her photography has been exhibited in galleries throughout the New York and New Jersey area.
