Make it yours.

  1. 01

    Get a feel for it.

    Drag the EOS to change your selfie perspective. Scroll or pinch to tighten the framing. Reset returns the camera and selfie to center; turn off Follow camera angle for the original webcam frame. The Back view shows the same framing on the rear screen.

  2. 02

    Find your light.

    Click Enable camera and allow access. A compact capture screen keeps the live view, shutter, looks, mirror, and timer together. Open Settings for exposure and angle adjustments.

  3. 03

    Make a memory.

    Press the large shutter, click the model’s silver shutter button, or hit Space. Tap the photo thumbnail beside the shutter to review and download your JPEG. Previous and Next move through your saved frames. Exit turns the camera off.

The angle control applies a perspective-and-crop effect to your webcam image. Physically move your device for a genuinely different viewpoint. Photos stay in your browser; download your favorites before leaving or refreshing. If camera access is blocked inside an embedded view, open this page in a full browser tab.
